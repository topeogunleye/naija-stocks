### Solo-Founder Weekly Batching System

To execute this daily social plan without spending all day on social media:

1. **Sunday Batching (2.5 Hours):**
   * Write the 1 Blog Post (or use an AI draft tool to outline based on your expertise, then edit).
   * Script 7 short videos (TikToks/Reels) using the daily prompts above.
   * Record all 7 short videos in 45 minutes using your phone.
   * Write and schedule 7 Twitter threads/posts using native Twitter scheduling or a free tool like Buffer.
2. **Daily Execution (15 Minutes):**
   * Upload the pre-recorded TikTok/Reel/Short for that day.
   * Spend 10 minutes replying to comments and engaging in the replies of relevant diaspora finance accounts.
3. **Friday Review (20 Minutes):**
   * Check the waitlist spreadsheet for new signups, track referral numbers, and update the waitlist count publicly on social channels.
